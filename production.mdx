---
title: "Prepare for production"
description: "Agree the production environment and verify the workflow your clinicians will actually use."
---

Complete your sandbox workflow before arranging production access with your
Cortexa integration contact. Use the production origin, credentials, and consent
entry point supplied for your integration.

## Confirm the environment

| Item | Confirm with Cortexa |
| --- | --- |
| API origin | Production hostname and data-hosting arrangements for your deployment |
| Application | Production application ID, server-side key, and registered HTTPS callback |
| Clinician access | How clinicians sign in and reach the consent flow |
| Authorization | Workspaces, scopes, and entitlements required by your workflows |
| Providers | Audio, generation, attachment processing, and coding capabilities you plan to use |
| Request allowance | Application-level rate limit and any relevant plan/generation quotas |

Use environment-specific credentials and signing keys. Do not construct a
production hostname or assume sandbox grants and resource IDs transfer between
environments.

## Verify the clinician journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the correct clinician and workspace">
    Exercise consent and your callback, validate `state`, exchange the code, and
    store the grant with your clinician/workspace mapping. Confirm token renewal
    works for that grant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use representative inputs">
    Run the audio formats, transcript languages, templates, and file types your
    integration will offer. Check the actual outputs with your clinical testers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle processing and edits">
    Show pending and failed jobs clearly. Keep operation keys stable during
    retries. Handle document version conflicts without discarding reviewed text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save to the intended encounter">
    Let the clinician review the result. Verify that your EHR write-back uses
    the patient and encounter mapping for that session and does not create
    duplicate records on retries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Operate the integration

Keep the API key in your backend's secret store. Renew Partner JWTs based on
`expires_in`, and stop using grants that have been revoked or lost workspace
access. Request a new authorization when necessary.

Track response status, job failure codes, and `X-Cortexa-Request-Id` for support.
Clinical payloads and credentials should not be included in ordinary integration
logs. Honor `Retry-After` and back off polling as described in [Jobs](/jobs).

Your application owns the recording interface, progress UI, clinician review,
and EHR write-back. The current public contract does not include an embedded
widget, completion webhooks, or a vendor-specific EHR adapter.

See [Errors and retries](/errors) for recovery behavior and
[API capabilities](/capabilities) for the supported integration surface.
